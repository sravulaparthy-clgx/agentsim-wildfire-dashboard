    How to get the files

    Install Overture's command-line tool with pip install overturemaps, then download each theme for a box given as west,south,east,north. For the Santa Barbara front country:

    overturemaps download --bbox=-119.78,34.39,-119.56,34.51 \
  -f geojson --type=segment -o segments.geojson
overturemaps download --bbox=-119.78,34.39,-119.56,34.51 \
  -f geojson --type=building -o buildings.geojson
overturemaps download --bbox=-119.78,34.39,-119.56,34.51 \
  -f geojson --type=land_cover -o landcover.geojson
overturemaps download --bbox=-119.78,34.39,-119.56,34.51 \
  -f geojson --type=land_use -o landuse.geojson
overturemaps download --bbox=-119.78,34.39,-119.56,34.51 \
  -f geojson --type=place -o places.geojson

    Make the box about 1–2 km larger than the area you care about; the model trims the edges and uses roads that leave the box as evacuation exits. Keep it under about 25 km across. GeoJSON and GeoJSONSeq both work; for GeoParquet, re-export with -f geojson.

    Without building footprints, households are placed along residential streets. Without land cover, fuels are inferred from terrain and building density. Land use adds irrigated parks, golf courses, orchards and vineyards. Places of interest (schools, hospitals, fire stations and so on) are optional; they appear as clickable markers with their own wildfire score.

      Wildfire scores and retrace

      Every Overture building and place gets an illustrative 0 to 100 score from its fuel and slope, its time to an exit, whether this run's fire reached it, and whether the households there got out. Click a row to open it on the map; the card shows each term and the Overture ID to join back to the source data.

      A run record stores the fire, weather and alert settings plus a checksum of every household's outcome. Replaying it on the same inputs must reproduce that checksum. The GeoJSON export skips buildings below the score filter and keeps each Overture ID.

      Scores are illustrative and uncalibrated. They rank sites within this model run; they are not probabilities of loss or an assessment of any real building. Place roles come from category keywords, and shelter candidates have not been checked for capacity or access.

      Assumptions, mismatches and limits

        Uploaded inputs may be real or illustrative. When sourced from Overture Maps, roads, buildings, land cover and land use retain their individual attribution and licensing terms. Elevation is requested from open Terrain Tiles (AWS Open Data, derived from USGS 3DEP and SRTM); a failed request uses flat terrain. Basemaps are from Esri, OpenTopoMap and OpenStreetMap.

        Modeled inputs: residential buildings become households (apartments count as several), sampled so each simulated household can stand for a few homes when the area is large. Zones are a 2 km grid unless uploaded polygons are provided; neither option is official without review. Fuels are coarse and inferred when land cover is absent. Exits are modeled where major roads leave the uploaded area. Fire behavior, alert reach, departure delays and road capacities are illustrative and uncalibrated. This is not an incident forecast or an official plan.
